The Artist

Ambassador of
Indigenous Sound

Born in the village of Masemola (Thabampshe) in Limpopo Province, South Africa, Volley Nchabeleng has spent over two decades travelling the world as a proud ambassador of African indigenous knowledge and sound.

A multi-talented percussionist, composer, producer and singer — Volley studied music at the Sibikwa Arts Centre and the University of South Africa (UNISA). His musical genius draws from the world's diverse sounds while remaining deeply rooted in Africa's pre-colonial heritage.

He has collaborated with creatives across six continents, worked alongside legendary director William Kentridge, and contributed his artistry to major television productions including the acclaimed DStv series Shaka Ilembe.
